# 🛍️ Retail Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI

A retail sales analysis project built using **SQL and Power BI** to understand sales performance across regions, stores, product categories, customers, and payment methods.

The project uses transaction-level retail data from **Omniretail Pvt. Ltd.** and turns the raw data into useful business insights through SQL analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard. 

## 📊 Project Overview

The main goal of this project was to understand how different parts of the retail business are performing and identify areas that could be improved.

The analysis focuses on:

* Regional sales performance
* Product category performance
* Product returns
* Store performance
* Customer spending
* Payment methods
* Quarterly sales trends
* Product pricing
* Top-selling products

## 🎯 Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

1. Which region has the highest total revenue?
2. Which product category generates the highest average revenue per sale?
3. What is the return rate for each product category?
4. Which 5 products have the highest total quantity sold?
5. Which store has the lowest revenue but the highest number of sales?
6. How do different payment methods affect total revenue?
7. Which customers have spent the most?
8. Which quarter has the highest sales?
9. What is the average unit price for each product category?
10. Which product categories have the highest return percentage?

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Differences in performance between regions
* High-performing and underperforming product categories
* Products with higher return rates
* Stores with unusual sales/revenue patterns
* Customers contributing the most revenue
* Payment methods associated with higher sales
* Periods with stronger sales performance

## 🎯 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, possible business recommendations include:

* Investigate underperforming regions and stores.
* Review products with unusually high return rates.
* Focus on high-performing product categories.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns and retain high-value customers.
* Encourage effective and convenient payment methods.
* Review store-level sales and profitability differences.
* Use quarterly sales trends for better inventory and promotional planning.
